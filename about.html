Our Story

Artha means purpose. That's the point.

In Sanskrit, artha is one of the four aims of a well-lived life — purpose and prosperity, pursued honestly. We named a coffee house after it because that's exactly what we set out to build: a place with a clear reason to exist, on a street that already loves good food and good coffee, but has never had both in one address.

The Idea

Every café nearby owns one moment. We wanted the whole day.

Defence Colony Road already has a strong specialty coffee and dining cluster — a European bakery-café, a rooftop coffee bar, an evening-only vegetarian restaurant, a French patisserie, a vegan café. Each does one thing very well, and stops there.

Artha was built to be the one address that runs from the first filter coffee of the morning through an Andhra lunch, an unhurried afternoon pour, and a rooftop evening of tandoor and small plates — a genuine destination café across three connected levels, not a passing counter.

Who's Behind It

Nineteen years of selling F&B. Eight years of fixing it.

Smt. Sri Lakshmi Tatavarthi

Founder & Proprietor

Sri Lakshmi brings 19+ years of continuous, salaried F&B experience across ITC Kakatiya, Ohri's Group, and Marasa Sarovar Premiere — where she currently leads sales. Artha is a proprietorship extension of that career, not a first-time leap: she owns Artha's guest experience, vendor relationships, and the financial discipline that keeps a kitchen honest day after day.

Sri Teja Behara

Operational Director

Teja brings 8+ years of food-technology operations from Uber Eats, DoorDash and Laalsa — direct, hands-on exposure to why cafés and restaurants fail operationally. He holds a PGDM in Operations Management from IMT Ghaziabad and is a Lean Six Sigma Green Belt. He is also an active specialty coffee practitioner — V60, Clever Dripper, cold brew — and designed Artha's brew bar programme and floor relay system by hand.

At The Brew Bar

Built around one machine, on purpose.

The brew bar is anchored by a Victoria Arduino Eagle One — a two-group Italian espresso machine with per-group temperature control, chosen for one reason above all others: shot-to-shot consistency. Premium coffee pricing only survives repeat visits if the cup tastes the same every single time.

It's also the machine our Head Barista owns end-to-end — from calibration to the full brew bar SOP. In a market where trained baristas are scarce, giving someone real ownership of serious equipment is how craft gets built into a team, not just a menu.

How We Run The Kitchen

Vegetarian, electric, and built to last.

Fully electric kitchen

No LPG connection — induction cooking throughout, more energy-efficient than the gas kitchen this address ran under its previous operator.

Vegetarian, seriously

A fully vegetarian kitchen, including egg-based dishes prepared on dedicated equipment with clear FSSAI green/brown-dot labelling.

Waste segregated at source

Wet and dry waste sorted across every floor, with used coffee grounds set aside for composting partnerships.

Water conscious

The RO system feeding our brew bar reuses reject water for cleaning, alongside low-flow fixtures across the kitchen.

Sourced close to home

Coffee comes from a Hyderabad-based roaster, and kitchen supplies from Hyderabad vendors wherever feasible.

Packaging that breaks down

Any takeaway coffee service uses compostable or recyclable cups, even though dining here is table-service first.
