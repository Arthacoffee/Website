The Only Full-Day Address On Defence Colony Road

Specialty coffee and a serious vegetarian kitchen, under one roof.

Five specialty coffee operators and a handful of well-loved restaurants sit within 400 metres of Artha — each of them owns a single occasion: a coffee counter, an evening thali, a bakery case. Artha is built to run the entire day, across three connected levels with lift access, from the first filter coffee to the last rooftop tandoor plate.

Built Deliberately
