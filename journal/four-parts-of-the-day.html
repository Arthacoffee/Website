One Address, Four Parts Of The Day

Artha Journal

Walk down Defence Colony Road in Sainikpuri and you'll find a genuinely good specialty coffee and dining cluster — a European bakery-café next door, a rooftop coffee bar a hundred metres on, a beloved evening-only vegetarian restaurant a little further, a French patisserie, a fully vegan café. It's a strong street. But look closely and every single one of them owns exactly one moment in the day.

The bakery-café does coffee and pastry, and stops there. The evening restaurant doesn't open until 7pm and serves no coffee at all. The rooftop coffee bar has no real meal programme. Nobody on this street runs the whole day.

So we built the whole day

Artha is organised around four distinct windows, each with its own character, menu emphasis, and pace:

10am – 12:30pm — Brunch & Brew Bar

The morning belongs to coffee — filter, V60, cold brew — alongside a light brunch menu: punugulu, avocado toast, shakshuka, pesarattu. This is the slowest, most deliberate part of the day.

12pm – 3pm — Andhra Traditional

Lunch is the everyday Andhra meal — rice plates, gravies, a proper thali — for the working professionals and students who make this street their weekday routine.

3pm – 6:30pm — The Afternoon Pour

The quiet window. Mostly coffee — filter and espresso — and the best time to sit with one of the brew bar's tasting flights.

6:30pm – 11pm — Tandoor, Bistro & Rooftop

Evenings shift upstairs. Italian pasta, Indo-Chinese, the evening tandoor and bistro small plates, with the rooftop terrace as the natural place to spend a longer evening.

Nobody comes to Artha for a quick stop on the way somewhere else. They come to spend a part of their day here — and we built the menu, the floors, and the rooftop around that.

That's the whole idea behind the name, and behind three connected floors instead of one counter. If you haven't been in yet, come find the part of the day that's yours.
